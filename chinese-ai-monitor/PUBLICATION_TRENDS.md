# Chinese AI Company Publication Trends (2025)

Based on analysis of publication patterns from January to December 2025, here are the key trends:

## Primary Publication Channels

### 1. **Gitee (China's GitHub)**
- **Why**: Aligned with local regulations, preferred by Chinese developers
- **Usage**: Many companies publish code repositories here first or exclusively
- **Example**: DeepSeek announced open-sourcing 5 repositories in February 2025
- **Note**: Over 10 million repositories, 5+ million users

### 2. **GitHub**
- **Why**: Global reach, international developer community
- **Usage**: Code repositories, releases, open-source projects
- **Example**: Alibaba's Qwen codebase, DeepSeek repositories
- **Timing**: Often published alongside or after Gitee releases

### 3. **Hugging Face**
- **Why**: Standard platform for model distribution
- **Usage**: Model weights, inference code, model cards
- **Example**: Rednote's dots.llm1, Qwen series models
- **Timing**: Usually after initial announcement

### 4. **ModelScope (Alibaba's Platform)**
- **Why**: China's largest AI open-source community
- **Usage**: 70,000+ models, 16 million developers (as of June 2025)
- **Example**: Alibaba's own models, many Chinese AI models
- **Note**: Major platform that should be monitored

### 5. **arXiv**
- **Why**: Academic credibility, technical details
- **Usage**: Research papers, methodology documentation
- **Timing**: Often published before or alongside model releases

### 6. **Company Blogs/Websites**
- **Why**: Official announcements, marketing
- **Usage**: Initial announcements, major releases
- **Timing**: Usually first, before technical details

## Publication Patterns (2025 Trends)

### Timing Patterns
- **Q1 (Jan-Mar)**: DeepSeek-R1 released in January, DeepSeek open-source announcement in February
- **Q2 (Apr-Jun)**: Continued open-source releases, Rednote models
- **Q3 (Jul-Sep)**: Alibaba releases in July, Qwen-3-Max-Preview in September
- **Q4 (Oct-Dec)**: Year-end releases, model updates

### Strategic Trends

1. **Open-Source First Strategy**
   - Chinese companies are aggressively open-sourcing models
   - By September 2025, Chinese models accounted for ~40% of global AI models
   - Cost advantage: Chinese LLMs cost ~1/5 of foreign counterparts

2. **Multi-Platform Publishing**
   - Companies publish on multiple platforms simultaneously
   - Gitee for domestic audience, GitHub for international
   - Hugging Face for model distribution, arXiv for papers

3. **Rapid Iteration**
   - Multiple model versions released throughout the year
   - Quick updates and improvements
   - Community feedback integration

4. **Cost-Effective Positioning**
   - Emphasis on affordability
   - Open-source to build market share
   - Focus on developer adoption
